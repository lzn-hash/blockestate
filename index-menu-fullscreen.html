Building the bridge
between two worlds.

— Our conviction

Over the past ten years, the crypto ecosystem has come of age. Decentralised finance (DeFi) and on-chain payment rails have reached a maturity that makes instant, secure and cross-border transactions a daily reality.

Yet acquiring a real-world asset — and a piece of real estate in particular — directly in crypto is still an obstacle course. Legal frictions, regulatory constraints, notaries and developers who don't yet speak the language: the barriers are real.

Our job at BlockEstate is to tear those barriers down. We offer our clients a turnkey solution and full support throughout the acquisition of their villa — whether they settle in crypto or in fiat.

— Three commitments

A promise
delivered end to end.

◆

Built real

No floating tokens, no abstract promises. Every one of our projects is land acquired, permits filed, a site supervised. Blockchain serves bricks and mortar — it doesn't replace them.

⬡

Frictionless payment

BTC, USDC, USDT. Settled through our licensed crypto payment partner, with instant conversion and notarial escrow. You pay in crypto, the seller receives fiat — with no volatility exposure.

◇

On-the-ground presence

We're physically on site. Site visits, finishes, key handover — every step is handled by our local team. Crypto makes the money move instantly; the relationship stays human.
